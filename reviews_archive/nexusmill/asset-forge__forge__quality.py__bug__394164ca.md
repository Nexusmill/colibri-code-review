Every prior remediation claim is present at the bytes (GROK-QLTY6's per-axis nan guard, the max-axis detail metric, the clamped saturation denominator, the conditional saturation floor). The defect is a contract gap the file's own docstring names without seeing: the floors were calibrated on full-bleed textures ("the 48-image reference set") and average over the whole frame, yet both callers apply them by default to emblem renders on the flat backdrop the product itself prompted for.

## Bugs & vulnerabilities

**[HIGH] The floor averages over the backdrop, so an emblem on the requested white/black backdrop (or a natively transparent RGBA) is judged on the backdrop, not the subject - the paid transparency is then skipped and bundles buy a second render** - `line 107` (`measure`) / `line 141` (`check`)
- What: every metric is a frame-wide mean/percentile (`g.mean()`, `np.percentile(g, ...)`, `np.abs(np.diff(g)).mean()`, `sat.mean()`). Emblem mode's default background is `transparent`, which the callers render as "isolated on a plain flat white backdrop" (library_gen: `prompt_fragment(opts)`, key white first). The backdrop is 60-95 % of the frame, so `detail` is diluted by the subject's coverage and `luminance` is pulled to the backdrop's value. `convert("RGB")` on a natively transparent RGBA (the Studio bundle path, AF-BUNDLE-ALPHA native transparency) discards the alpha and judges the hidden RGB under the clear pixels (black from every common encoder).
- Trigger: any emblem-mode run with the floor at its default (`run_job(quality_floor=True)`, `build_bundle(quality_floor=True)` - neither gates on mode) whose subject covers less than roughly 40 % of the frame, or any natively transparent output whose opaque area is under ~20 %. Measured at 512 px: a shaded motif that scores detail 11.8 as a full-bleed texture scores 3.6 (< 4.5) at 28 % coverage on white; a 5 % motif also reads luminance 248 on white and 12 on black; the 12 % RGBA reads luminance 18, detail 1.6, saturation 0.047 - three floors at once.
- Impact: library_gen records `quality_warning`, moves the paid file to `flagged/` and - by design of LG-5 - skips the dual-pass alpha solve, so the transparency the user paid for is never produced and the flagged file is the opaque white-backdrop render; bundle.py spends a SECOND paid render at the alternate seed (its retained one-retry), which fails the same way, then flags. Money and the product contract both.
- Fix (proposed, verified GREEN on a scratch copy of the package): `measure(path_or_image, backdrop=None)` / `check(..., backdrop=None)` judge the SUBJECT - the alpha channel when the image carries one (explicit intent, never a guess), else the pixels whose RGB distance from the backdrop the caller says it asked for exceeds `BACKDROP_TOL` (0.12, the same key alpha_by_removal uses), and only when the frame's corners actually show that backdrop (median within 0.15), so a model that ignored the request falls back to the full-frame metrics unchanged. A frame with under 0.5 % subject is the genuine degenerate case and fails as "empty frame". Callers pass the hint: library_gen `"white"` for transparent (the first frame) or the chosen white/black; bundle the chosen white/black (native RGBA needs none). Full-bleed callers pass nothing and get exactly the old numbers. Battery `probe_af_quality_1.py` (suggested `tests/harness/probes/sweep_af_quality_r6.py`, tag `specs:probes-sweep-af-qf-r6`): 7 of 8 checks RED on the repo bytes, 8/8 GREEN on the patched copy; `af_output_knobs.py`, `grok_oo_output.py`, `lg_money_resume.py` GREEN on the patched copy.
- Verification: CONFIRMED - trigger traced from both callers' defaults through `check` to the frame-wide means, reproduced through `run_job` with the fake provider (item flagged, alpha skipped, 1 call instead of 2). No on-disk emblem job record exists on this machine to quantify the live rate (63 job records scanned, all texture runs); the synthetic coverage sweep bounds it.

## Missing safeguards
- A very pale subject on a white backdrop (or a near-black one on black) can read as "empty" under the new mask (distance ≤ 0.12) - the full-frame floors would have failed such an image anyway (blown-out / no structure), so this is not a regression, but the empty-frame reason will be the visible message. PLAUSIBLE, not exercised.
- `measure` resizes non-square frames to 512x512 (aspect ignored); the detail metric is mildly anisotropic under that squash. Bundles allow aspect ratios; libraries are 1:1. Not a defect at the floor's margins.
- `MIN_SATURATION` is still calibrated on a 9-image spot check (the file says so); nothing here changes that.
- Out of unit, one line: `bundle.py:263-269` still performs the one-retry-at-a-new-seed that HM-EXCISE (2026-08-12, Damien: "we shouldn't auto fix these images") removed from library_gen - a second paid render on every floor failure, including the false ones above. For the bundle.py reviewer.

## Adversarial verification pass (refuted claims)
- "A corrupt PNG through `Image.open` crashes a paid run" - refuted: library_gen wraps `check` (GROK-LG5 #1: a crash is recorded as a floor failure, file kept); bundle's `_build_one` try trips the all-or-nothing Stop by design.
- "`im.resize((512, 512))` loses the 1px guard" - refuted: a 1-px source resizes to 512 only when `max(size) > 512`; a 1xN with N > 512 becomes 512x512 (no nan); a 1x1 stays 1x1 and the per-axis guard returns 0.0.
- "`Image.open` leaves a handle open on Windows, blocking the flagged/ rename" - refuted: `convert()` forces `load()`, and Pillow closes an exclusively-opened fp after load; the rename is on the coordinator thread after the worker returned.
- "`prompt_wants_colour` misses 'grey' / 'gray'" - refuted as a defect: COLIBRI-LIB-1's catalog stamp (`expect_colour` on the item) is the authority in library_gen; the sniff is the fallback and the markers are deliberately the ones the pools use.
- "16-bit 'I' mode sources clip to white in `convert('RGB')`" - refuted for this product: providers return 8-bit PNG/WebP/JPEG; no path feeds a 16-bit source to the floor.
