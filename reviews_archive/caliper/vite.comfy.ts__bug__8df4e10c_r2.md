## Verdict
The 25 lines themselves are unchanged and their own residual (silent parse fallback) sits on the owner's known shelf — but the lineage hunt falsifies the file's founding contract: the header's "The one place the ComfyUI neighbor is named" (line 1) is untrue at these bytes.
